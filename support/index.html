Frequently asked questions

Can I play online with another person?

No. The public game is offline and played against a bot. Any online alpha is private testing only.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is required. The game is designed to play offline.

Does the game have ads or purchases?

No. The game has no ads or in-game purchases.

Where can I download Ironveil?

It is in development; no public store link is available yet. Check the Release page for updates.

How do I report a bug?

When a support email is published, send a short description of what happened, what you expected, steps to reproduce it, your device and OS version, and whether it happened more than once. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal data. Add a screenshot only if it contains no private information.
