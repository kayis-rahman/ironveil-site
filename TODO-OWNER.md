# Owner tasks before launch

The site is a static preview, not ready to publish as-is. Supply and verify these owner-only details:

- [ ] Legal developer/person or business name for the copyright line and privacy policy.
- [ ] Monitored support and privacy contact email; choose a realistic response expectation.
- [ ] Controller identity and privacy-policy effective/update date; review policy against the exact release and hosting setup.
- [ ] Determine whether EU trader declaration or other local disclosure applies; supply postal/address and registration/VAT details only when required.
- [ ] Confirm final app/platform/device/OS claims, support documentation, game copy and privacy statements against the actual release.
- [ ] Add verified TestFlight and App Store URLs only when those builds and pages exist; publish real version notes and requirements.
- [ ] Create approved gameplay media (video or screenshot walkthrough), captions/transcript and accessible description; review any third-party video provider/privacy implications before embedding.
- [ ] Decide whether the game has launched before changing the release/demo copy or status.
- [ ] Choose and purchase a domain only when ready; configure DNS and GitHub Pages settings, then update the sitemap/robots sitemap URL and social image absolute URL as needed. Never add `CNAME` until the domain is chosen and configured.
- [ ] Review copyright, licenses, product name/trademark and final imagery before launch.
