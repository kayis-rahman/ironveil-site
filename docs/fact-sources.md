## Gameplay rules

Gameplay copy on Home (`index.html`), How to Play (`how-to-play/index.html`) and Demo (`demo/index.html`) was checked against the current rules implementation in the read-only `projects/ironveil` repository. Source paths below are relative to that repository; line ranges identify the implementation or approved game-design contract.

- **Offline match and rival temperaments:** `docs/plans/offline-mvp.md:3-6,33-37` describes the game against a bot and keeps online multiplayer separate; `client/Core/RivalBrain.cs:7-20` defines Balanced, Raider, Gatherer and Turtle. This supports the offline bot and temperament-selection copy on Home, How to Play and Demo.
- **Playable gods and their powers:** `client/Core/Characters.cs:6-18,24-34,41-54` defines Freya, Thor and Loki, their costs and scout duration. `client/Core/Rules.cs:316-360,628-687` verifies the once-per-turn bonus action, ability validation and decoy placement; `client/Core/Rules.cs:645-665` verifies Thor's two-level shelter damage from any range and Freya's private three-round scout. These support the corresponding Home and How to Play descriptions.
- **Season order, duration and survival effects:** `client/Core/Rules.cs:22,115-125,881-903` sets the round 56 limit, the summer/autumn/winter/thaw schedule with three winters, and survival endings when a player's health reaches zero from exposure. `client/Core/Balance.cs:40-54` defines seasonal cold, warmth and food/wood/fire availability. These support the season and winter descriptions on Home, How to Play and Demo.
- **Veil and scouting:** `docs/design/game-design-decisions.md:50-61` records that rival position remains trackable while survival details and inventory are hidden outside visibility, with Freya's scout and Ansuz as exceptions. `client/Core/Rules.cs:660-665,710-717,735-737` implements the private scout and inventory-revealing rune. This supports the Veil and clue/scouting descriptions on Home and How to Play.
- **Rune powers and invocation:** `client/Core/MatchState.cs:50-68` lists the six rune types; `client/Core/Balance.cs:56-66` sets their spawn cadence and the three-rune invocation cost (any types in the current profile); `client/Core/Rules.cs:238-254,700-747` verifies invocation and effects. This supports the six rune effects and cost described in How to Play.
- **Victory paths:** Survival by striking a rival or exposure is implemented at `client/Core/Rules.cs:547-550,897-903`. Dominance's two-resource lead across three types, checked after gathering or stealing, is implemented at `client/Core/Balance.cs:105-109` and `client/Core/Rules.cs:552-555,824-826`. Ragnarok's two-grid-step range, two-rune/two-food cost and two consecutive turns including its start are set at `client/Core/Balance.cs:111-121` and implemented at `client/Core/Rules.cs:82-95,256-272,591-621`. These support the victory-path copy on Home, How to Play and Demo.
